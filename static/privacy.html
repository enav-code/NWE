Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, Keeply receives the basic profile information Google shares, such as your name, email address, profile image, and account identifier. We also store the assets, documents, people, and reminders you choose to add.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, display your workspace, save your records, provide reminders, and operate and secure Keeply. We do not sell your personal information.

Documents and uploads

Files you upload are stored for your workspace. Keeply may process their contents to extract useful fields such as item names, vendors, prices, and dates. Avoid uploading information you do not have permission to store.

Sharing

We share information only when needed to operate the service, when you choose to share a workspace, when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of Keeply and its users.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect workspace data. No online service can promise absolute security. You may request deletion of your workspace data by contacting the workspace owner.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as Keeply develops. The current version will be posted here. Questions can be sent to the Keeply workspace owner.
